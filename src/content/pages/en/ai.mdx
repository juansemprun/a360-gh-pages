---
title: Our stance on AI
description: At Air360, we use AI only where it matters. Our hybrid approach blends predictive analytics, custom models, and clear insights to solve real UX problems. Because AI should work for you, not the other way around.
---

At Air360, we decided not slapping AI on everything. Here's why.

Yes, AI has made incredible progress in the recent years.  
LLMs have reshaped the world and they're cool. We get it.  
However, AI everywhere isn't always better. In fact, it's often worse.  
Many companies jumped to integrate ChatGPT just to join the cool kids' club. 

Can it help pinpoint where the problem is? Yes, provided the data is correct.  
Can AI tell you why users abandon your checkout? No.  
That's where Air360 shines.  


<strong>
To solve this, Air360 takes a hybrid approach:
</strong>

1. All analytics calculations use predictive methods and verifiable math.
2. Proactive monitoring relies on neural networks and proven algorithms.
3. LLMs explain complex data in plain, simple language.
4. Everything runs on our infrastructure with our own customized models.
5. Zero third-party integration. Your data is too important to share, period.

So when we get the question **"What's your AI strategy?"**, here's our answer:  
We use AI where it creates real impact for users.  
We build tools that solve problem, not for the hype.  


<strong>
The bottom line?
</strong>

Yes, we use AI in Air360. Neural networks, LLMs, the works.  
We're constantly exploring how new research can help our customers.  
But here's what we won't do: **oversell it**.  
AI is a tool, not magic.  
And we believe this is how you build trust that lasts.
