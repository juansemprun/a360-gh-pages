faqs:
  - question: What is User Path Optimization?
    answer: User path optimization helps you understand how users move through your website and identify where they get confused, distracted, or lost. By analyzing these paths, you can remove friction and guide users more smoothly toward key actions.
  - question: How can user path optimization help reduce drop-offs?
    answer: User path optimization helps you understand what users do immediately after they leave an important step in your flow. By seeing where they go, you can identify pages that distract them, areas where information is unclear, or moments where the call-to-action may not be strong enough. It also reveals navigation patterns that might confuse users. Once you understand the specific points where people hesitate or abandon the process, you can address those issues directly and reduce drop-offs more effectively.
  - question: What’s the difference between a funnel and user journey feature in user path optimization?
    answer: Funnels track a predefined, linear process (e.g., product page → cart → checkout). User journeys, on the other hand, reveal actual, non-linear behavior—including loops, backtracking, detours, and alternative routes users take before converting or dropping off. By combining both features, you can se the whole picture of how the users moved through out your website. Together, they give you a complete picture of how users truly experience your site.
  - question: Can I segment the analysis by audience type?
    answer: Yes, you can. Funnels and user journeys can be filtered by different audience characteristics such as device, country, region, traffic source, campaign, and whether someone is a new or returning visitor. This allows you to compare how various groups behave and understand the differences in their paths. With this insight, you can optimize the experience for each audience segment based on their unique behavior.
  - question: How do I validate my findings from a journey analysis?
    answer: You can instantly open session replays for any step or path you’re analyzing. This allows you to watch real user interactions, confirm your assumptions, and understand the context behind behaviors, making your optimization decisions more accurate.
