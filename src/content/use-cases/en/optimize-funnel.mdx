faqs:
  - question: What is funnel optimization?
    answer: Funnel optimization is the process of analyzing and improving each step of your conversion path to reduce drop-offs and increase the percentage of users who complete desired actions.
  - question: How do I identify funnel bottlenecks?
    answer: Use Air360's Funnel Analysis to visualize drop-off rates at each step. Session Replays show you exactly why users abandon the funnel, revealing friction points and usability issues.
  - question: What's a good funnel conversion rate?
    answer: It varies by industry and funnel complexity. E-commerce checkouts typically see 20-30% conversion, while multi-step lead generation might be 5-15%. Focus on improving your baseline, not just hitting industry averages.
  - question: Should I optimize the entire funnel at once?
    answer: No, focus on the step with the highest drop-off rate first. Make data-driven improvements one step at a time, measure results, then move to the next bottleneck.
  - question: How do I reduce form abandonment?
    answer: Simplify forms by removing unnecessary fields, add progress indicators, provide inline validation, enable autofill, and clearly explain why you need each piece of information.
  - question: Can I track users who return to complete a funnel later?
    answer: Yes! Air360 tracks cross-session behavior, showing you when users start a funnel in one session and complete it later, helping you understand the full conversion journey.
