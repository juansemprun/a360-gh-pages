  - question: How do I know which content needs optimization?
    answer: Use Air360's heatmaps and zoning analysis to identify content areas with low engagement. Focus on high-traffic pages with poor conversion rates or high bounce rates.
  - question: What metrics should I track for content performance?
    answer: Track scroll depth, time on page, click-through rates on CTAs, engagement with specific content zones, and conversion rates to understand how your content performs.
  - question: How can I test different content variations?
    answer: Use Air360's Snapshots feature to establish a baseline before making changes. Then compare performance after updates to measure the impact of your optimizations.
  - question: Should I optimize for mobile differently than desktop?
    answer: Yes! Mobile users have different behaviors and smaller screens. Use Air360's device-specific analytics to optimize content layout, length, and CTAs for each platform.
  - question: How often should I optimize my content?
    answer: Review high-impact pages quarterly, and monitor key pages monthly. Use Air360's alerts to catch sudden drops in engagement that require immediate attention.
  - question: Can I see which headlines perform best?
    answer: Yes! Air360's zoning analysis and session replays show you exactly how users interact with headlines, images, and other content elements so you can optimize what matters most.
