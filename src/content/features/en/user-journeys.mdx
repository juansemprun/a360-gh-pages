faqs:
  - question: What are User Journeys?
    answer: User Journeys visualize the complete path users take through your website, from their first page view to conversion or exit, showing you every touchpoint and interaction along the way.
  - question: How is User Journeys different from Funnel Analysis?
    answer: While Funnel Analysis tracks predefined conversion paths, User Journeys reveal organic, unexpected paths users actually take, helping you discover new optimization opportunities and understand real user behavior.
  - question: Can I segment User Journeys by user type?
    answer: Yes! Segment journeys by traffic source, device type, user location, new vs. returning visitors, conversion status, and custom user attributes to understand how different audiences navigate your site.
  - question: How many pages can a User Journey include?
    answer: User Journeys can track unlimited page views and interactions within a session, giving you a complete picture of even the most complex user paths through your website.
  - question: Can I identify common journey patterns?
    answer: Absolutely! Air360 automatically identifies the most common journey patterns, showing you which paths lead to conversions, which lead to exits, and opportunities to guide users toward your goals.
  - question: How do User Journeys help with content strategy?
    answer: User Journeys reveal which content users engage with, in what order, and how it influences their decisions, helping you optimize content placement, navigation, and calls-to-action.
