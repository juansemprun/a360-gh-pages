faqs:
  - question: What are Snapshots?
    answer: Snapshots are point-in-time captures of your website's analytics data and design, allowing you to preserve and compare performance metrics before and after design changes, A/B tests, or major updates.
  - question: What data is included in a Snapshot?
    answer: Snapshots capture all analytics data including heatmaps, session replays, funnel performance, user journeys, and behavioral metrics for the selected time period and pages.
  - question: How many Snapshots can I create?
    answer: You can create unlimited Snapshots depending on your plan. Each Snapshot is stored independently, allowing you to build a comprehensive history of your website's evolution and performance.
  - question: Can I compare Snapshots side-by-side?
    answer: Yes! Air360's comparison view lets you analyze two or more Snapshots side-by-side, with automated detection of significant changes in key metrics and visual differences in user behavior.
  - question: When should I create a Snapshot?
    answer: Create Snapshots before major redesigns, A/B tests, feature launches, marketing campaigns, or seasonal changes to establish baselines and measure the impact of your changes.
  - question: Can I share Snapshots with stakeholders?
    answer: Absolutely! Export Snapshots as reports or share secure links with team members and stakeholders, complete with annotations and insights to communicate findings effectively.
