faqs:
  - question: What are Snapshots?
    answer: Snapshot preserves past versions of your website, so you can compare how design changes affect user behavior and performance with ease in Air360. Think of it as a timeline for your site.
  - question: Why use Snapshot?
    answer: You can open the saved design to analyze page and element performance easily using Air360's extension. Measure the performance of before/after, and show stakeholders clear evidence of improvement.
  - question: How does Snapshot work?
    answer: Save a snapshot with a few click whenever you make a change or want to evaluate a specific page version. We store the visual design, making it easy to compare different versions over time.
  - question: When should I create a Snapshot?
    answer: Create Snapshots before major redesigns, A/B tests, feature launches, marketing campaigns, or seasonal changes to establish baselines and measure the impact of your changes.
  - question: How does Snapshot support optimization strategy?
    answer: Compare performance before and after changes to see what actually drives results. When you can prove which design improved conversions or reduced drop-offs, you make confident decisions backed by evidence. Snapshot gives you the data to justify investments, scale what works, and avoid repeating past mistakes.
