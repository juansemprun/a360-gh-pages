  - question: What is Funnel Analysis?
    answer: Funnel Analysis tracks users through a series of steps toward a conversion goal, showing you exactly where and why users drop off, helping you optimize each stage of the journey.
  - question: How many steps can a funnel have?
    answer: You can create funnels with unlimited steps, from simple 2-step funnels to complex multi-stage conversion paths. Air360 automatically tracks progress and drop-off rates at each stage.
  - question: Can I compare different funnels?
    answer: Yes! You can create multiple funnels and compare their performance side-by-side, analyze different user segments, or compare time periods to track improvement over time.
  - question: What types of funnels can I track?
    answer: Track any conversion funnel including checkout processes, signup flows, lead generation forms, content engagement paths, app onboarding, and custom user journeys specific to your business.
  - question: How does Funnel Analysis handle different user paths?
    answer: Funnel Analysis intelligently tracks both linear and non-linear paths, showing you alternative routes users take and identifying which paths lead to the highest conversion rates.
  - question: Can I set up automatic alerts for funnel performance?
    answer: Yes! Set up alerts to notify you when drop-off rates exceed certain thresholds, when conversion rates change significantly, or when specific funnel steps show unusual behavior.
