    answer: Zoning Analysis is a powerful feature that helps you visualize user behavior by dividing your page into interactive zones, allowing you to identify high-performing areas and optimize your layout.
  - question: How does Zoning Analysis work?
    answer: Zoning Analysis uses advanced heatmap technology to track user interactions across different zones on your page, providing detailed insights into which areas capture the most attention.
  - question: Can I customize the zones?
    answer: Yes! You can create custom zones based on your specific layout and business needs, allowing for flexible and targeted analysis.
  - question: What metrics does Zoning Analysis track?
    answer: Zoning Analysis tracks clicks, hovers, scroll depth, time spent in each zone, and conversion rates, giving you a comprehensive view of user engagement.
